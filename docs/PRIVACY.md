# Privacy policy
EnterLib collects nothing. No accounts, advertising SDKs, analytics or crash reporters. Permissions: INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE.
Stored only on your device: catalogue metadata, watchlist, playback progress, sync history, and your source URLs/tokens (Android
encrypted preferences where the device supports it). Network requests go only to servers you configure, to TMDB (if you add a key),
and to artwork hosts named in your catalogue. Backups are files you export yourself. Cleartext HTTP is permitted for LAN servers;
use HTTPS when you can. External streaming providers may show their own ads outside EnterLib's control.
